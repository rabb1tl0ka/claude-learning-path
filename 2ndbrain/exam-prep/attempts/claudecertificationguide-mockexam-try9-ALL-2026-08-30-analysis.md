## Failure patterns

**1. Structured per-component state over raw logs/text markers, for handoffs and resumption.** Two of the five misses (Q32, Q53) share the same shape: when a component needs to hand state to another (a crashed pipeline resuming, a synthesis agent tracing claims to sources), the tempting "safe" answer is to carry forward the raw conversation log or embed markers in prose — it feels lossless. But raw logs aren't addressable per-unit, and inline text markers get silently detached the moment anything downstream reprocesses the text (paraphrases, merges, truncates it). The correct answer both times was: have each producing component emit its own **structured artifact** (a per-document report, a claim-source pair with real fields) that survives reprocessing. This is now drafted as a new cross-cutting heuristic in the survival guide (see below) — first appearance, evidence from a single attempt so far.

**2. Matching delegation to whether a piece that could do the job already exists — in both directions.** Q50 and Q60 are opposite-direction instances of the existing "missing capability vs. already-adequate piece" heuristic. Q50: the coordinator already held the context needed for a simple follow-up summary, and the fix was to use it directly rather than re-spawning a subagent (wrong instinct: optimize the unnecessary re-spawn with prompt caching, instead of asking whether the extra hop was needed at all). Q60: a single agent's context was genuinely degrading from breadth (45 files, losing accuracy after only 8), and the fix was to add the missing piece — subagent decomposition to preserve high-level context — rather than tuning the existing single-agent approach leaner (switching to Grep). Both are now logged as new worked examples under that heuristic.

**3. Other misses:** none — all 5 wrong answers fit one of the two patterns above; no unpatterned "other misses" this time. (Q48, the Edit-tool `replace_all` miss, is grouped under pattern 1's sibling gap-topic note rather than a new pattern — see coverage gaps below, since the exact trap was already documented from three prior attempts.)
